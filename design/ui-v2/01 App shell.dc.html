Archetype 01

App shell and navigation

Fixed sidebar, one-row top bar, and a content region that is the only thing that scrolls. Navigation, page purposes and the three-way theme control are unchanged from the current app; the chrome moves onto petrol-tinted neutrals, a 200px rail on surface rather than a near-black gradient, Hanken Grotesk for labels and JetBrains Mono for counts and freshness. The m7kni house mark is used as shipped.

Narrow shell

Below 768px the rail leaves the flow and returns as a drawer over a scrim. The top bar keeps the disclosure control, the title and the freshness label; the stat strip folds to two columns and keeps its dividing rules. Buttons grow to 32px so every target clears 44px with its padding.
