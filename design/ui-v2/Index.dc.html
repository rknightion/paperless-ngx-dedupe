Decisions worth knowing

The product violet is retired. Paused becomes warn, pending graph edges take chart series 1, and chart series 1 no longer needs to avoid looking like a button.

Confidence bands moved onto the status treatment. ■ HIGH 97.4% replaces the tinted pill, keeping the existing 0.90 and 0.75 thresholds.

The near-black rail is gone. The sidebar is a surface with a hairline and an accent inset edge on the active item, so it inverts with the rest of the app.

One contrast failure found and fixed. White text on the dark-mode destructive fill measures 3.01:1. Semantic fills take the on-accent token, which is dark ink in the dark scope.

The first-run checklist can now finish. Three real conditions, a collapsed dismissible row when they all pass, and a return only if one regresses.
