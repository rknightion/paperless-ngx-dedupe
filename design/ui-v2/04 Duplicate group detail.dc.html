The screen the product is for: is this a duplicate, and which copy do you keep. Confidence is stated three ways at once - shape, word and literal number (■ HIGH 97.4%) - so the band survives greyscale, colour blindness and a screenshot. The score is then decomposed into its three measures with their weights, because a 97% built from boilerplate is a different thing from a 97% built from an account number.

Below that, the comparison itself: the same fields in the same order on both sides, with every differing value flagged ≠ rather than left for the eye to find.
